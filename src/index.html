Inspect, then prune

Drag to orbit. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Two fingers, right-drag or Shift+drag pan across the crown. Switch the one-finger tool below if needed. Fit tree recenters the view.

Tap a shoot near its union for a collar cut. Tap farther along it for a heading cut. Near a side fork, the outgoing branch collar takes priority. The rooted stem is protected. Hide leaves for a clearer view.

Confirm only after checking the coral preview. Undo works until you advance the year. +1 year harvests the standing fruit and grows a new canopy in a single step.

A teaching model, not a yield forecast. Ages refer to completed growing seasons: last year's extension is one-year wood. Removal budgets use live shoot length, not fruit count.
